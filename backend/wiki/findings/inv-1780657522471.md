## Executive Summary

The investigation into the claim that Danish military equipment contributes to the conflict in Gaza has produced mixed results across several sub-claims. Some lines of inquiry, such as the involvement of Danish companies in the export of military equipment, show indirect connections via exports to Israel. However, direct evidence linking these exports to Gaza-specific conflicts is absent. The investigation has revealed high confidence in the use of Danish military items by other nations involved in Gaza and in these items' production through procurement programs. Financial and individual contributions by Danish entities display weaker connections, with significant evidence gaps identified.
